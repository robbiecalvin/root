Emergency Expense Loan Purpose

Emergency expense loans are designed for immediate, non-discretionary costs where timing is critical and delaying payment may worsen the situation. Typical use cases include urgent travel, temporary accommodation, utility restoration, or time-sensitive home and vehicle breakdowns.
